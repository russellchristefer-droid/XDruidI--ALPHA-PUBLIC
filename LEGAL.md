# Legal

XDruid I is a game you play in a web browser. This note is the legal section for the project.

## Privacy

The homestead is stored in the browser on that device (local storage). Playing does not require an account. The save is not uploaded as part of ordinary play. If you use a hosted copy that offers sign-in, that sign-in is separate from the yard save and follows that host's session cookie. Do not put passwords, recovery codes, or private facts in the yard, the pack notes, or the chat.

## Your device

Open the game in a browser on a phone or a computer. Do not expect a phone to launch it as a separate application from a bare file link. Use the site address.

## Third-party art

The fish and fishing-rod icons included in the game are third-party pixel art. They are used only as part of this game: the rod in the farmer's hand, and the fish he can take from the pond. Those pictures are not offered as a standalone pack, stock library, or direct download for reuse. Do not extract them and redistribute them on their own. Other game art stays with this project and is not a separate asset store.

## Disclaimer

The game is an alpha. It is provided as a work in progress. The yard, the gates, the spells, and the days can change between saves. There is no warranty, and no promise that a save from this build will load unchanged in a later one. You are responsible for the device and browser you play on.

## Acceptable use

Do not attack the host, other players, or the repository. Do not try to break into accounts, scrape private data, or ship exploit code. Security reports go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
