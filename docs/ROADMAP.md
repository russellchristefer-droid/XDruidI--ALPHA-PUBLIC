# Public Roadmap

**Dated:** 2026-11-10 (REL-001)
**Hyper-realism check:** Committed items are only those already shipped or accepted as work orders. Aspirational items are clearly labeled and do not appear in release notes as complete.

## Committed (shipped or accepted)

These are present in the public changelog or the verified repository:

- Homestead yard, beds, animals (cow, rooster, goat, sow, cat), fishing pond, magic courtyard.
- Portals to Svarga, Naraka, Skill Grove, Combat, Quarry, Floor 332, Mansion foundation, and other lands.
- Pack of 28 slots with weight; tools spend Floor.
- Day/night clock, weather, autonomy, needs decay.
- Local save with checksum, backups, quarantine (SAVE-005/006).
- Opt-in local telemetry.
- Localization scaffolding (English source).
- Modding registry (typed definition files, versioned API).
- Public changelog page.
- CI: typecheck, lint, tests on every change.
- Error boundaries and structured error log.

## Aspirational (not scheduled, not promised)

Drawn from the design pillars in AGENTS.project.md. These are direction, not commitments:

- Full life-cycle stages, heir/legacy system.
- Deeper metabolism, wounds, spoilage, and fair survival pressure.
- Aspiration, wants/fears, memory log that shape mood.
- Tile-level terraforming and quality-rated construction (Wurm-inspired).
- Interconnected production economy with depletion and market prices.
- Paper-doll equipment with meaningful affixes and sets.
- Seasonal art variants (winter/snow) once commissioned or derived under the gap policy.
- Additional realms and ritual content via the modding registry.

Nothing in the aspirational list appears in version numbers or release notes until it is turned into an accepted work order and shipped.
